Collaborative Workspace

#3 Verify · Nov 2025

Signature Verification

A Siamese CNN that learns 4096-dimensional embeddings to tell genuine signatures from forgeries — 93% accuracy, 0.93 F1.

TensorFlowKerasOpenCVPython

Source ↗
Details
  • 3.3M-parameter shared-weight encoder in TensorFlow/Keras.
  • OpenCV preprocessing with CLAHE contrast enhancement.
  • Trained on 23,206 signature pairs with binary cross-entropy and Adam; evaluated on 5,748 pairs.
